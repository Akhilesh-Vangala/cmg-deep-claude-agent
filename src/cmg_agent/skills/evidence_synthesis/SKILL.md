# Skill: Evidence Synthesis

## Purpose
Summarize clinical-trial evidence relevant to a drug comparison without overstating efficacy.

## When to use
- Requests for clinical trial landscape or supporting evidence
- Comparative briefings for medical-affairs review

## Procedure
1. Call `trials_search` with drug names and oncology context.
2. Record NCT IDs, titles, status, and URLs.
3. Synthesize a neutral summary of available trials.
4. Never claim superiority without an explicit comparative study citation.

## Constraints
- Trials listed are informational pointers, not efficacy conclusions.
- Flag conflicting or incomplete records for human review.
